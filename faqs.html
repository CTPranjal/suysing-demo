Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about ordering, delivery, and your store account.

Account & Sign Up

Who can register a Suy Sing store account?

Any active sari-sari store, mini-mart, or grocery retailer in the Philippines can register. You'll need basic store details and a valid ID to get verified.

How long does verification take?

Most accounts are verified within one business day once your documents are submitted and legible.

Can I have more than one store under one account?

Yes — you can add multiple store locations to a single account from your dashboard once verified.

Ordering

Is there a minimum order amount?

Minimums vary by branch and product category. Your order summary will always show the minimum before checkout.

Can I edit my order after placing it?

Orders can be edited or cancelled before they're marked "for delivery." After that, please contact your branch directly.

Delivery

How do I know which branch delivers to my area?

Use the branch locator on the Branches page — your account will automatically be linked to the nearest servicing branch.

What if I'm not around when my order arrives?

You can designate an authorized representative at sign-up to receive deliveries on your behalf.

Payments

What payment methods are accepted?

Cash on delivery, bank transfer, and major e-wallets are supported, depending on your branch.

Retail Academy

Is Retail Academy really free?

Yes, all courses are free for verified Suy Sing store accounts.
